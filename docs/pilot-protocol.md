# Proposed supervised pilot protocol

No active school pilot is claimed. Before recruitment or collection: identify accountable agronomic and implementation partners; obtain ethics/safeguarding and institutional approvals; document informed adult consent and appropriate child protections; minimize data; approve access, retention, incident, photography, and withdrawal procedures; calibrate sensors; train adults; and pre-register evaluation and stopping rules.

Start with synthetic tabletop tests, then non-child controlled garden testing. Advance only after safety gates. Record technical failures and adverse events, provide human review of alerts, never make children responsible for action, and publish only reviewed aggregates. Independent validation is required.
