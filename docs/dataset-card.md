# Dataset card

All 360 included observations are deterministic synthetic data generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py`: three non-identifying demo sites, 30 days at six-hour intervals, and normal, heat-stress, and low-soil-moisture scenarios. No child data, real farmer personal data, exact location, or identifying geography is included.

The generator uses seeded diurnal curves and scenario adjustments; it does not reproduce real field distributions, correlations, missingness, sensor error, climate, crops, or behavior. It cannot validate accuracy, safety, fairness, usability, or impact.

Real data may be incorporated only after consent and safeguarding review, institutional approval, minimization and retention decisions, role-based access, security review, partner accountability, documented provenance/license, quality assessment, de-identification review, and a controlled publication decision.
