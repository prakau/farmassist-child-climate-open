# Safeguarding

## Required principles and controls

- **Do no harm:** stop work when risk outweighs expected benefit.
- No direct child profiling and no child personal data, health data, biometrics, contacts, or identity linkage.
- Obtain informed adult participant consent and appropriate school/institutional approval; child-related activity needs independent safeguarding review.
- Minimize fields and retention; restrict operational access by role and review it regularly.
- Establish named incident escalation, reporting, containment, survivor-centered support, and non-retaliation procedures before a pilot.
- Publish only privacy-reviewed aggregates; suppress small groups and never publish precise locations.
- Field photography needs specific consent, safe framing, secure storage, withdrawal procedures, and no identifying metadata.
- Communicate alerts as uncertain prompts for trained adult review, never instructions to children.
- Models require local agronomic validation, human review, and safe fallback procedures.

Before real deployment, approve consent materials, retention/deletion schedules, access matrices, partner responsibilities, referral routes, and an incident-response drill.
